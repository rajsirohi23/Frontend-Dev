Typography and Text Styling Showcase

Typography plays a crucial role in web design, influencing how users perceive and read content. A good combination of font styles, sizes, and spacing can make an article more engaging and easy to follow. Designers often experiment with different font families to balance readability and visual appeal.

Modern web design emphasizes clarity, structure, and hierarchy. The line-height ensures comfortable reading, while letter-spacing improves text legibility. Aligning text properly—whether left, center, or justified—can change how readers interact with the content.

Remember, text styling is not just about aesthetics—it’s about creating a smooth reading experience. Effective use of emphasis tags like <strong>, <em>, and <mark> helps highlight important information.
